ORIGINS OF BLOOD MAGIC

The origin of Blood Magic is writer-defined. Two developed possibilities are available. They are alternatives a writer can choose from, adapt, or combine; neither is a universal explanation.

4.1 INTRINSIC BLOOD

In this origin, blood naturally possesses supernatural properties. Blood Magic exists because blood itself has an inherent magical nature.

In the version previously discussed, everyone can learn Blood Magic through practice. This is an example of how the origin could work, not a mandatory rule for every version of the system.

A writer using this origin decides what makes blood supernatural, why its properties exist, whether those properties differ between individuals or species, and how people first discovered how to use them.

4.2 THE PRIMORDIAL BLOOD CRYSTAL

This origin is tied to the greatest war in human history.

After the war, the blood, bodies, bones, and fragments of existence of most of humanity sank into wet, iron-rich earth. Over approximately one hundred years, under pressure, these remains combined and formed a gigantic crystal.

The centre of the crystal resembles a tiny star. However, rather than containing hydrogen and helium as a normal star would, its centre contains pure soul.

The crystal is extraordinarily durable. In the concept as described, it would take either a catastrophic strike to its core or approximately a quintillion years for its power to expire.

The crystal is a source of soul power. Almost anyone descended from those whose remains contributed to it could potentially inherit the ability to manipulate blood. The possibility also extends to descendants of other species if the remains of those species contributed to the crystal.

This origin leaves several matters open for a writer to decide:

  • - Whether and how living people can access the crystal's power directly.
  • - Whether the crystal is aware or has any form of consciousness.
  • - Whether the crystal permanently changes the bloodlines of those connected to it.
  • - Whether inherited ability is automatic, conditional, or variable.
  • - Whether other species have different forms of inheritance.
  • - How the crystal's soul power relates to individual blood and individual techniques.

These questions have not been given universal answers.

4.3 THE ORIGIN DOES NOT DEFINE EVERY MECHANIC

Even if a writer chooses one of these origins, the details of Blood Magic's operation remain separately customisable. The underlying supernatural nature of blood is writer-defined. A particular origin does not automatically settle every question about blood, materials, inheritance, control, or the five disciplines.
