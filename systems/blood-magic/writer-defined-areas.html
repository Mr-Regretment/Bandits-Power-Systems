WRITER-DEFINED AREAS AND DELIBERATE FLEXIBILITY

The following list gathers details intentionally left open. Their absence is not an oversight; they are areas in which different writers may reasonably want different mechanics.

18.1 SYSTEM-WIDE

  • - Whether everyone can learn Blood Magic.
  • - Whether practitioners inherit the ability.
  • - Whether particular species have different rules.
  • - Whether blood's supernatural properties are intrinsic or come from an external source.
  • - Whether blood is chemically ordinary.
  • - Whether blood can become or transform into another substance.
  • - Whether blood retains memories, personality, emotion, or biological information from its source.
  • - Whether blood can act autonomously.
  • - Whether blood can be preserved indefinitely.
  • - How much resistance targets can offer.
  • - Whether non-practitioners can resist manipulation.
  • - Whether every material can be penetrated.
  • - Whether materials can resist or nullify Blood Magic.
  • - Whether techniques require additional preparation.
  • - Whether Blood Magic can create matter or transmute materials.
  • - The precise physical consequences of overuse.

18.2 GENERAL BLOOD MANIPULATION

  • - Maximum range and quantity.
  • - Precision and number of independently controlled portions.
  • - The strength and duration of fluid barriers or shapes.
  • - The limits of controlling blood inside living organisms.
  • - How resistance affects control.
  • - How easily blood can be moved through or inside different materials.

18.3 BLOOD ARTILLERY

  • - Projectile dimensions and shape restrictions.
  • - Speed and range.
  • - Number of simultaneous projectiles.
  • - Required concentration and persistence.
  • - Splitting and recombination behaviour.
  • - Steering precision.
  • - Collision behaviour and final-collision rules.
  • - What happens when the projectile reaches its final collision.
  • - Blood quantity required.
  • - Whether other people's blood can be used for a particular version.
  • - Difficulty controlling unfamiliar blood.
  • - Number of mana spells that can be carried.
  • - Spell activation conditions.
  • - Compatibility between the projectile and the mana spell.

18.4 BLOOD ARMAMENT

  • - Which object properties can be altered.
  • - How blood quantity relates to those properties.
  • - Flexibility, hardness, repair, reshaping, movement, and recall.
  • - Material compatibility and resistance.
  • - Blood channels and their complexity.
  • - Object size, intricacy, and force limits.
  • - How many objects can be infused or managed.
  • - How attention is divided.
  • - Whether multiple practitioners can contribute.
  • - Whether others can control, drain, or replace infused blood.
  • - Whether objects can be repaired or reconstructed from fragments.
  • - Creator connection, sensing, and recall conditions.

18.5 BLOOD DOMINION

  • - The maximum distance and flexibility of the blood streams remain unspecified.
  • - Exact limits on the physical movements that can be mirrored are governed by the target's physical limitations; any additional exceptions would be writer-defined.
  • - Any broader variations beyond the retained mechanics can be customised, but the detailed current version is preserved in Chapter 9.

18.6 BLOOD COVENANT

  • - The detailed consequences of ambiguous or contradictory wording have not been separately specified.
  • - The precise range of possible punishments depends on the setting's laws and physical possibilities.
  • - Particular forms of terms, completion conditions, and duration are determined by each Covenant.
  • - The social use of Covenants is worldbuilding-dependent.

18.7 BLOOD RELIQUARY

  • - How information is stored.
  • - What information source is used.
  • - Capacity and number of stored individuals.
  • - Number of simultaneous reconstructions.
  • - Duration.
  • - Degree of independent thought and initiative.
  • - Damage, restoration, and healing.
  • - Exact reconstruction method.
  • - Whether physical capabilities can be altered from the original.

18.8 BLOOD CRYSTALS

  • - Whether crystals exist in a given setting.
  • - How artificial crystals are made.
  • - The potency of different materials.
  • - The availability and effects of natural crystals.
  • - The practical meaning and consequences of increased maximum blood capacity.
