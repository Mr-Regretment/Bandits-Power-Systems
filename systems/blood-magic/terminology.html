CORE TERMINOLOGY

BLOOD MAGIC

The overall system of techniques that use physical blood as their required medium.

GENERAL BLOOD MANIPULATION

The foundational ability to directly control blood as a substance. It is not one of the five formal disciplines, but is the basic form of Blood Magic.

BLOOD CONTROL

An informal description previously used for the direct, blood-bending aspect of General Blood Manipulation. The preferred system term is General Blood Manipulation.

BLOOD ARTILLERY

The discipline based on launching and directing blood as a projectile, with the ability to interact with mana-based magic in compatible settings.

BLOOD ARMAMENT

The discipline of infusing materials with blood so that crafted objects can gain personalised physical properties and a connection to their creator.

BLOOD DOMINION

The discipline that links a practitioner's movements to selected muscle groups in another person, causing those muscle groups to mirror the practitioner's corresponding movements.

BLOOD COVENANT

The discipline that creates supernatural agreements between participants and enforces the terms of those agreements.

BLOOD RELIQUARY

The discipline that allows a practitioner to store information about individuals they personally killed using Blood-based techniques and later reconstruct those individuals as compelled, non-resurrected recreations.

BLOOD INFUSION

A working term for introducing blood into a material as part of Blood Armament. It describes the process, not a separate discipline.

INFUSED ARMAMENT

A working term for an object that has undergone Blood Armament. Such an object remains the material object it was: for example, a steel sword infused with blood remains a steel sword containing blood. It is not automatically a separate kind of entity or a generic "blood construct."

PRIMORDIAL BLOOD CRYSTAL

One optional origin concept: a gigantic crystal formed from the remains of those who died in a catastrophic war. Its centre contains pure soul rather than ordinary stellar fuel.

HEART-SHAPED BLOOD CRYSTALS

Crystals associated with replenishing blood. In the established optional crystal concept, artificial and naturally occurring varieties have different effects.
