Foundations

GENERAL BLOOD MANIPULATION

6.1 IDENTITY

General Blood Manipulation is the most basic form of Blood Magic. It is not one of the five formal disciplines, but it is the foundation on which the other disciplines are understood.

It is direct control of blood as a fluid or substance. It is broadly similar to the idea of bloodbending: manipulating blood itself rather than invoking one of the specialised disciplines.

It is not powerless merely because it is foundational. It is versatile and can produce a wide variety of physical results, provided the practitioner can control the blood with sufficient precision.

6.2 DEFAULT CAPABILITIES

General Blood Manipulation can include:

  • - Moving blood from one place to another.
  • - Changing its shape and distribution.
  • - Concentrating blood in selected areas.
  • - Forming fluid structures, including walls and barriers.
  • - Manipulating blood inside living organisms.
  • - Controlling blood that has entered or remains inside materials.
  • - Directly applying physical force through controlled blood.

The precise limits and applications of these abilities can be changed by a writer.

6.3 LIVING BLOOD

General Blood Manipulation can control blood inside living people in the default mechanical conception.

The fact that a technique can do this does not mean it is socially accepted. Manipulating living blood is generally forbidden in the default social framing, though writers may change that law or custom.

The target's ability to resist, the practitioner's familiarity with the blood, and any relevant technique-specific rules influence how this works in a particular setting.

6.4 SHAPING BLOOD

A practitioner can shape blood into fluid forms, including barriers or walls. These remain blood-based forms rather than automatically becoming solid objects with the properties of another material.

The stability, strength, size, duration, and physical behaviour of such forms are writer-defined.

6.5 THE BOUNDARY WITH BLOOD ARMAMENT

General Blood Manipulation can theoretically produce unusual physical results by exerting force or by using extraordinarily precise control. However, reshaping or changing the physical properties of non-blood materials through General Blood Manipulation is not its practical speciality.

Blood Armament exists to solve that problem. It provides a specialised, repeatable way to infuse materials with blood and change their physical properties without requiring extreme precision from the practitioner.

Blood Armament is the only formal discipline whose defining speciality is changing the physical properties of objects and materials.

General Blood Manipulation is therefore versatile, while Blood Armament is the focused and practical method for material alteration. The two are not distinguished simply by one being stronger than the other.
