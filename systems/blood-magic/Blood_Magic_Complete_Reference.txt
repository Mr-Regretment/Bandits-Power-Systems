1. PURPOSE AND DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
======================================================================

Blood Magic is a standalone magic system built around the manipulation
and use of physical blood. It is not a renamed version of a conventional
mana system, and it does not require the mechanics or terminology of a
game system.

The system has a foundational form of direct blood manipulation and five
formal disciplines:

  - General Blood Manipulation
  - Blood Artillery
  - Blood Armament
  - Blood Dominion
  - Blood Covenant
  - Blood Reliquary

General Blood Manipulation is the basic, foundational form rather than a
sixth formal discipline. The five named disciplines each have a more
specialised identity.

The system is intended to be useful to writers. It therefore establishes
clear default foundations while leaving many implementation details to
the writer. A writer should be able to use the system as presented
without having to invent every rule, but should also be able to adapt
particular mechanics to suit a setting or story.

The system is not exclusively a combat system. Its techniques can have
applications in craftsmanship, manufacturing, medicine, architecture,
transportation, communication, law, research, education, and ordinary
daily life, depending on the world in which it exists.

The five disciplines have distinct identities. They should not be
interchangeable collections of generic powers. Their exact mechanics
may be customised, but their defining concepts should remain recognisable
unless a writer deliberately changes the system itself.

No generic mana pool, Blood Unit, spell-slot system, core, arbitrary rank
structure, or numerical reserve is required. Blood availability is
understood in terms of actual blood and the particular technique being
used, rather than a universal points currency.

2. HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT: RULE CATEGORIES
======================================================================

The system uses three categories of rules.

A. UNIVERSAL FOUNDATIONS

These are the broad principles that define the system as currently
agreed. They describe what Blood Magic is at its most fundamental level.

B. DEFAULT MECHANICS

These are the standard assumptions and behaviours recommended for a
normal version of the system. They provide a working baseline, but a
writer may change or remove them.

C. WRITER-DEFINED MECHANICS AND OPTIONAL APPLICATIONS

These are details that deliberately remain open. The document may give
examples of how they could work, including mechanics from earlier
versions of the design, but those examples are not universal rules.

Where a section describes a specific earlier implementation, it is
identified as an example or historical prototype. Do not mistake a
prototype's exact numbers or limitations for mandatory rules.

A further distinction is important: a persistent magical effect is not
the same thing as blood acting autonomously. For example, an object
altered through Blood Armament can retain its altered properties without
the practitioner continuously concentrating on it. That does not mean
that a pool of blood can independently decide to move or perform a new
action after the practitioner's control has ended.

3. CORE TERMINOLOGY
======================================================================

BLOOD MAGIC

The overall system of techniques that use physical blood as their
required medium.

GENERAL BLOOD MANIPULATION

The foundational ability to directly control blood as a substance. It is
not one of the five formal disciplines, but is the basic form of Blood
Magic.

BLOOD CONTROL

An informal description previously used for the direct, blood-bending
aspect of General Blood Manipulation. The preferred system term is
General Blood Manipulation.

BLOOD ARTILLERY

The discipline based on launching and directing blood as a projectile,
with the ability to interact with mana-based magic in compatible
settings.

BLOOD ARMAMENT

The discipline of infusing materials with blood so that crafted objects
can gain personalised physical properties and a connection to their
creator.

BLOOD DOMINION

The discipline that links a practitioner's movements to selected muscle
groups in another person, causing those muscle groups to mirror the
practitioner's corresponding movements.

BLOOD COVENANT

The discipline that creates supernatural agreements between participants
and enforces the terms of those agreements.

BLOOD RELIQUARY

The discipline that allows a practitioner to store information about
individuals they personally killed using Blood-based techniques and
later reconstruct those individuals as compelled, non-resurrected
recreations.

BLOOD INFUSION

A working term for introducing blood into a material as part of Blood
Armament. It describes the process, not a separate discipline.

INFUSED ARMAMENT

A working term for an object that has undergone Blood Armament. Such an
object remains the material object it was: for example, a steel sword
infused with blood remains a steel sword containing blood. It is not
automatically a separate kind of entity or a generic "blood construct."

PRIMORDIAL BLOOD CRYSTAL

One optional origin concept: a gigantic crystal formed from the remains
of those who died in a catastrophic war. Its centre contains pure soul
rather than ordinary stellar fuel.

HEART-SHAPED BLOOD CRYSTALS

Crystals associated with replenishing blood. In the established optional
crystal concept, artificial and naturally occurring varieties have
different effects.

4. ORIGINS OF BLOOD MAGIC
======================================================================

The origin of Blood Magic is writer-defined. Two developed possibilities
are available. They are alternatives a writer can choose from, adapt, or
combine; neither is a universal explanation.

4.1 INTRINSIC BLOOD

In this origin, blood naturally possesses supernatural properties.
Blood Magic exists because blood itself has an inherent magical nature.

In the version previously discussed, everyone can learn Blood Magic
through practice. This is an example of how the origin could work, not a
mandatory rule for every version of the system.

A writer using this origin decides what makes blood supernatural, why
its properties exist, whether those properties differ between
individuals or species, and how people first discovered how to use them.

4.2 THE PRIMORDIAL BLOOD CRYSTAL

This origin is tied to the greatest war in human history.

After the war, the blood, bodies, bones, and fragments of existence of
most of humanity sank into wet, iron-rich earth. Over approximately one
hundred years, under pressure, these remains combined and formed a
gigantic crystal.

The centre of the crystal resembles a tiny star. However, rather than
containing hydrogen and helium as a normal star would, its centre
contains pure soul.

The crystal is extraordinarily durable. In the concept as described, it
would take either a catastrophic strike to its core or approximately a
quintillion years for its power to expire.

The crystal is a source of soul power. Almost anyone descended from
those whose remains contributed to it could potentially inherit the
ability to manipulate blood. The possibility also extends to descendants
of other species if the remains of those species contributed to the
crystal.

This origin leaves several matters open for a writer to decide:

  - Whether and how living people can access the crystal's power
    directly.
  - Whether the crystal is aware or has any form of consciousness.
  - Whether the crystal permanently changes the bloodlines of those
    connected to it.
  - Whether inherited ability is automatic, conditional, or variable.
  - Whether other species have different forms of inheritance.
  - How the crystal's soul power relates to individual blood and
    individual techniques.

These questions have not been given universal answers.

4.3 THE ORIGIN DOES NOT DEFINE EVERY MECHANIC

Even if a writer chooses one of these origins, the details of Blood
Magic's operation remain separately customisable. The underlying
supernatural nature of blood is writer-defined. A particular origin does
not automatically settle every question about blood, materials,
inheritance, control, or the five disciplines.

5. UNIVERSAL FOUNDATIONS AND GENERAL RULES
======================================================================

5.1 PHYSICAL BLOOD IS REQUIRED

Every Blood Magic technique requires physical blood.

This is a defining foundation of the system: Blood Magic uses blood as
its medium. A technique may use blood in different ways, such as a
projectile, an infusion, a connection, a mark, or a reconstructed body,
but physical blood is required for the technique to function.

The amount and source of blood required can vary by technique.

The requirement for physical blood does not mean that every technique
must use the practitioner's own blood. Whether a technique can use the
blood of another person, the practitioner's own blood, or either source
is a separate matter. The default position is that other people's blood
can be used, although unfamiliar blood is generally harder to control.

5.2 BLOOD AVAILABILITY IS A LIMITING FACTOR

The amount of blood a practitioner can access is a central practical
limitation.

A technique's scale, duration, complexity, or demands may be constrained
by the quantity of blood available. Different techniques can have
different blood requirements. There is no universal numerical Blood
Unit or fixed points pool.

Writers may introduce alternative sources, replenishment methods, or
other ways of managing blood availability. They may also change or
remove the ordinary limitations if that better suits the setting.

5.3 PHYSICAL STRAIN

Using too much of one's own blood can physically weaken or harm the
practitioner. Excessive blood loss can have serious bodily consequences.

Overexerting one's ability to perform Blood Magic can also cause strain.
The earlier design described this metaphorically as "spraining" one's
blood: pushing beyond one's ability can cause pain, difficulty, or
failure.

The exact symptoms, thresholds, recovery, and consequences are not
universal. Writers may define them differently. This rule is a default
limitation, not a required medical model.

5.4 ACTIVE CONSCIOUS CONTROL

The default rule is that blood must be actively and consciously
controlled to move or perform an action through direct manipulation.
When the practitioner stops controlling it, the blood does not continue
performing that action on its own.

Writers may allow blood to retain instructions, act autonomously, or
continue carrying out functions without active attention. Such behaviour
is an optional variation, not the default.

This rule does not prevent a technique from leaving behind a persistent
magical result. An object whose physical properties have been altered by
Blood Armament can remain altered without the practitioner continuously
controlling the blood inside it. A Covenant can remain binding after the
formation technique ends. Such persistence belongs to the effect of the
technique; it does not mean that the blood independently chooses new
actions.

5.5 BLOOD CAN COME FROM DIFFERENT SOURCES

By default, practitioners can manipulate blood from themselves or other
people. A practitioner's own blood is generally more familiar and easier
to work with.

Other people's blood is usually less familiar. The comparison used in
the design was like driving an unfamiliar car: the practitioner can
operate it, but may not intuitively understand its particular behaviour
as well as their own.

This unfamiliarity is not necessarily active resistance by the blood's
owner. It is a control and familiarity issue.

Writers may change or remove the distinction between familiar and
unfamiliar blood.

5.6 BLOOD CAN ENTER AND REMAIN INSIDE MATERIALS

The default system allows blood to enter materials and remain inside
them. It can exert physical forces from within a material or object.

This capability is especially important to Blood Armament, but is not
limited to the discipline's defining identity. General Blood
Manipulation can also interact with blood that has entered a material.

The default model treats the blood as a substance that can occupy the
inside of another material. The precise physical and chemical
relationship between blood and the material is writer-defined.

5.7 PENETRATING MATERIALS

The developed default concept is that Blood Magic can force blood into
materials, moving it through fibres, gaps, pores, or other available
structures. Density and material structure affect how difficult this is.

In the earlier design, magic could force absorption even where ordinary
liquid would not naturally enter. More resistant or dense materials
could be harder to penetrate.

Writers can change whether all materials can be penetrated, whether
some materials are immune or resistant, and how material structure
changes the difficulty.

5.8 DISTRIBUTION AND CONCENTRATION

Blood can be evenly distributed or concentrated in particular areas.
A practitioner is not necessarily restricted to keeping all controlled
blood in one uniform mass.

Blood can also enter a material or target from multiple points of
contact, where a technique permits it.

The degree of precision, number of independently controlled portions,
and difficulty of complex distributions are writer-defined.

5.9 THE PHYSICAL AND CHEMICAL NATURE OF BLOOD

This is deliberately writer-defined.

A writer decides whether blood remains physically and chemically
ordinary blood, whether magic changes its physical or chemical
properties, whether it can transform into other substances, and whether
it possesses additional supernatural properties beyond being
manipulable.

The system does not require every setting to use the same answer.

5.10 BLOOD AGE AND CONDITION

The recommended default is that age alone does not automatically make
blood unusable. Its physical condition may matter more than its age.

Fresh, dried, contaminated, preserved, or otherwise altered blood may
behave differently depending on the world's rules.

Whether old blood retains magical usefulness, whether blood naturally
deteriorates for magical purposes, and how preservation works are
writer-defined.

5.11 COMBINING BLOOD FROM DIFFERENT PEOPLE

The recommended default is that blood from different people can be
combined. There is no universal incompatibility that makes combination
impossible.

Controlling a mixture may be more difficult because it contains blood
from multiple sources and may involve unfamiliar patterns of control.

Writers decide whether the sources remain distinguishable, whether
mixing changes the blood's properties, and how difficult combined
control becomes.

5.12 RESISTANCE AND COUNTERING

As a default, another practitioner may be able to resist or interfere
with attempts to manipulate their blood. The degree and nature of that
resistance are not fully fixed.

The design distinguishes between unfamiliarity and resistance:

  - Unfamiliarity means a practitioner has less intuitive control over
    another person's blood.
  - Resistance means the target or another practitioner actively
    opposes the manipulation, where the setting allows this.

Writers decide whether non-practitioners can resist, how powerful
resistance can be, and whether particular techniques bypass or overcome
it.

5.13 BLOOD AND ITS SOURCE

The relationship between blood and the person or being it came from is
entirely writer-defined.

Blood may or may not retain a supernatural connection to its source.
Possible forms of connection include biological information, memories,
emotional traces, personality, behavioural tendencies, or a link to the
original owner. None of these possibilities is compulsory.

5.14 SPONTANEOUS USE AND PREPARATION

Blood Magic can be used spontaneously. Mandatory preparation is not a
universal requirement.

Some techniques may require preparation, marks, infusions, agreements,
or other prior steps. These requirements belong to the particular
technique rather than to Blood Magic as a whole.

5.15 MATERIAL LIMITATIONS AND PHYSICAL REALITY

Blood Magic does not automatically imply that matter can be created
from nothing. The earlier design treated the use of existing materials
as the basis for crafting and reshaping objects.

Whether a writer permits material creation, transmutation, or other
exceptions is a worldbuilding decision. Blood Armament's established
identity is the alteration of physical properties in existing crafted
objects, not the automatic creation of matter.

5.16 NO UNIVERSAL NUMERICAL RESOURCE POOL

Blood availability is measured conceptually through the blood actually
available and the demands of the technique. No standardised Blood Unit,
mana pool, spell slot, or other numerical reserve is required.

5.17 FORBIDDEN TECHNIQUES

Manipulating blood inside living people is generally forbidden in the
default social and legal framing. The prohibition is comparable in
seriousness to a war crime in the setting that uses this assumption.

This is a societal or legal principle, not an intrinsic magical barrier.
Writers can change, weaken, or remove the prohibition. A setting might
also have supernatural systems that enforce legal restrictions, such as
a mandatory Blood Covenant.

6. GENERAL BLOOD MANIPULATION
======================================================================

6.1 IDENTITY

General Blood Manipulation is the most basic form of Blood Magic. It is
not one of the five formal disciplines, but it is the foundation on
which the other disciplines are understood.

It is direct control of blood as a fluid or substance. It is broadly
similar to the idea of bloodbending: manipulating blood itself rather
than invoking one of the specialised disciplines.

It is not powerless merely because it is foundational. It is versatile
and can produce a wide variety of physical results, provided the
practitioner can control the blood with sufficient precision.

6.2 DEFAULT CAPABILITIES

General Blood Manipulation can include:

  - Moving blood from one place to another.
  - Changing its shape and distribution.
  - Concentrating blood in selected areas.
  - Forming fluid structures, including walls and barriers.
  - Manipulating blood inside living organisms.
  - Controlling blood that has entered or remains inside materials.
  - Directly applying physical force through controlled blood.

The precise limits and applications of these abilities can be changed
by a writer.

6.3 LIVING BLOOD

General Blood Manipulation can control blood inside living people in
the default mechanical conception.

The fact that a technique can do this does not mean it is socially
accepted. Manipulating living blood is generally forbidden in the
default social framing, though writers may change that law or custom.

The target's ability to resist, the practitioner's familiarity with the
blood, and any relevant technique-specific rules influence how this
works in a particular setting.

6.4 SHAPING BLOOD

A practitioner can shape blood into fluid forms, including barriers or
walls. These remain blood-based forms rather than automatically
becoming solid objects with the properties of another material.

The stability, strength, size, duration, and physical behaviour of such
forms are writer-defined.

6.5 THE BOUNDARY WITH BLOOD ARMAMENT

General Blood Manipulation can theoretically produce unusual physical
results by exerting force or by using extraordinarily precise control.
However, reshaping or changing the physical properties of non-blood
materials through General Blood Manipulation is not its practical
speciality.

Blood Armament exists to solve that problem. It provides a specialised,
repeatable way to infuse materials with blood and change their physical
properties without requiring extreme precision from the practitioner.

Blood Armament is the only formal discipline whose defining speciality
is changing the physical properties of objects and materials.

General Blood Manipulation is therefore versatile, while Blood Armament
is the focused and practical method for material alteration. The two
are not distinguished simply by one being stronger than the other.

7. BLOOD ARTILLERY
======================================================================

7.1 CORE IDENTITY

Blood Artillery is the discipline of launching blood as a projectile and
directing it after launch.

Its defining ideas are:

  - Blood is launched as a projectile.
  - The practitioner can direct the projectile.
  - The projectile can interact with mana-based magic in compatible
    versions of the system.

This identity is more important than any one projectile size, speed,
range, or collision rule.

7.2 THE PROJECTILE

The basic concept is that a practitioner expels or otherwise uses blood,
shapes it into a projectile, and launches it.

The projectile remains blood-based. Its shape, size, speed, range,
number, persistence, and exact physical behaviour are writer-defined.

Other people's blood can be used in the broader system by default, but
how easily unfamiliar blood can be shaped into an Artillery projectile
is writer-defined.

7.3 DIRECTION AND CONTROL

The practitioner can direct the projectile. The extent of steering,
precision, required concentration, and whether control can be shared or
interrupted are writer-defined.

7.4 COLLISIONS AND DISPERSAL

The way a projectile behaves when it hits an obstacle or target is
writer-defined. A version may have the blood bend around an obstacle,
smash through it, disperse, split, or behave in another way.

A version may allow the practitioner to choose which collision counts
as the projectile's final collision. What happens at that collision is
also writer-defined.

7.5 INTERACTION WITH MANA-BASED MAGIC

Blood Artillery can interact with mana-based magic. An established
example is placing a mana spell inside a blood projectile so that the
projectile carries the spell while travelling.

The exact interface between the two systems remains writer-defined.
The Artillery discipline does not inherently make Blood Magic dependent
on mana.

7.6 EARLIER PROJECTILE IMPLEMENTATION (OPTIONAL EXAMPLE)

The following mechanics appeared in an earlier detailed version. They
are preserved as an example of one possible implementation, not as
mandatory rules:

  - The practitioner expelled their own blood and shaped it into a
    projectile.
  - The projectile could take any shape within a bounding box no larger
    than 0.75 metres on each side.
  - The blood remained liquid and generally tended to come back together
    after separating, subject to the particular collision or final-impact
    rules of that implementation.
  - When encountering a wall or obstacle, it could either smash through
    or bend around it.
  - The practitioner could freely steer it during flight.
  - The practitioner selected which collision counted as the final
    collision.
  - At the final collision, the blood splashed apart and became
    ordinary, uncontrolled blood.
  - A larger projectile required more blood.
  - The original version included a mana-spell payload, with no more
    than one mana spell inside a projectile.
  - The caster could activate the contained spell remotely at any point
    during flight, or arrange for it to activate automatically on the
    selected final collision if it had not already been activated.

The exact projectile size, the one-spell limit, the use of the
practitioner's own blood, speed, range, simultaneous projectile count,
concentration, persistence, splitting, collision rules, and activation
conditions are all writer-defined in the current broad design.

7.7 MANA SPELL PAYLOAD: INTERFACE EXAMPLE

The separate mana system previously discussed has a mechanism in which
a practitioner contains a mana spell and embeds it in a compatible
mystical object. The first activation by a user while using or being at
the object consumes the stored activation without requiring the user's
mana. That stored activation is used once; subsequent uses require the
ordinary mana cost, cast at the object.

Blood Artillery's possible use of this mechanic is an example of the
two systems interacting. The exact compatibility rules, containment
method, activation conditions, and number of spells per projectile
remain writer-defined.

8. BLOOD ARMAMENT
======================================================================

8.1 CORE IDENTITY

Blood Armament is the discipline of infusing materials with blood to
give crafted objects personalised physical properties and a connection
to their creator.

It is an extension of conventional craftsmanship, not simply a method
of conjuring magical weapons.

Blood Armament is the only formal disciple whose defining speciality is
changing the physical properties of objects and materials.

8.2 DEFINING PROPERTY

Blood Armament always allows blood to change an object's physical
properties.

This is the defining feature of the discipline. The particular
properties changed, how they change, and any additional capabilities
are writer-defined.

8.3 INFUSION AND THE MATERIAL

In the established default concept, blood enters a material and remains
inside it. The object remains the object it originally was. A steel
sword infused with blood is still a steel sword containing blood; it is
not automatically a separate entity or a generic "blood construct."

The blood can be manipulated inside the object. It can exert force from
within and can alter the object's physical behaviour according to the
rules chosen by the writer.

The exact physical or chemical relationship between the blood and the
material is writer-defined under the general rules of Blood Magic.

8.4 PERSISTENCE

A central established capability is that infused blood can remain in
an object without the practitioner continuously concentrating on it.

The resulting altered properties can persist as an effect of Blood
Armament. This does not mean the blood must autonomously perform new
actions. Any active movement or adjustment of blood inside the object
can still require conscious control under the default rule.

8.5 CONNECTION TO THE CREATOR

Blood Armament gives the infused object a connection to its creator.
The exact nature and strength of that connection are writer-defined.

Possible applications include a sense of the object's location or
condition, an advantage in controlling its infused blood, or the ability
to recall it. None of these examples is compulsory in every version.

8.6 WRITER-DEFINED PROPERTIES AND APPLICATIONS

Writers decide:

  - Which physical properties are altered.
  - How those properties are altered.
  - Whether the object gains additional capabilities.
  - How much blood is needed for different objects.
  - Whether blood quantity affects flexibility, hardness, or other
    properties.
  - Whether the object can become flexible, move, repair itself, reshape
    itself, or be recalled.
  - Whether the object can sense or communicate its location or
    condition to its creator.
  - Whether multiple people can contribute blood to one object.
  - Whether another practitioner can take control of the infused blood.
  - Whether infused blood can be replaced with blood from another
    source.
  - Whether blood can bridge damage or gaps in an object.
  - Whether fragments can be reconstructed.
  - How many objects can be infused or controlled at once.
  - How attention is divided between several objects.
  - Which materials are compatible and how easily they accept blood.
  - How object size, intricacy, complexity, and material affect the
    process.
  - How much force an infused object can exert.
  - How many special properties one object can possess.
  - The range and conditions of any recall ability.
  - Whether recall requires sight and whether weight or anchors matter.

8.7 EARLIER MATERIAL-PROPERTY MODEL (OPTIONAL EXAMPLE)

An earlier detailed implementation explored the following model. These
are examples, not universal requirements:

  - Blood penetrated materials through their fibres, pores, gaps, or
    other structures; density and construction affected the difficulty.
  - Blood remained inside the object as a separate liquid substance.
  - More blood could make an object softer or more flexible, while less
    blood could make it harder or stiffer.
  - A flexible sword could be stiffened on command.
  - Blood itself did not become rigid; the material supplied the
    object's hardness.
  - Internal blood channels could be created by skilled practitioners.
  - Blood could move through those channels when consciously controlled.
  - Blood could exert internal force, allowing an object to flex, twist,
    move, or reshape.
  - A healing-channel arrangement could allow an object to repair
    damage. More blood could improve repair while reducing hardness.
  - Reshaping had no established absolute limit in theory, but material,
    size, and intricacy made a difference in practice.
  - An object could be reshaped back to an earlier form.
  - Multiple materials, such as wood, metal, and string, could be
    combined; ease depended on the materials' structure and ability to
    accept blood.
  - Multiple practitioners could contribute blood to the same object.
  - Another practitioner could potentially drain or take over infused
    blood, while the current user could counter that attempt.
  - If the blood escaped from an object, the properties it provided
    could be lost. The blood might be siphoned back into the practitioner.
  - Replacing an object's infused blood with blood from another source
    could be difficult, like reverse-engineering an unfamiliar system.
  - A damaged object could be bent back into shape. Fluid blood might
    bridge a missing-material gap, although that area would be weaker.
  - Fragments could preserve enough of an object's outline or geometry
    to allow progressive reconstruction.
  - Living materials such as wood, leather, or bone could be infused.
  - Controlling multiple objects was possible, but divided attention
    could make it difficult.

8.8 EARLIER RECALL EXAMPLE (OPTIONAL)

One earlier version explored a recall ability reminiscent of a weapon
returning to its owner. In that example:

  - Recall from 0 to 10 feet could work without sight.
  - Recall from 10 to 20 feet required sight.
  - Recall beyond 20 feet was unavailable.
  - Heavier objects were more difficult to recall.
  - Anchors or opposing forces could prevent or challenge recall.
  - The creator had a faint intuitive sense of the object's location or
    condition.

These distances and restrictions are not universal. Recall itself is
an optional application of the creator connection.

8.9 FAMILIARITY AND CONTROL BY OTHERS

An earlier model gave the creator an advantage because they were
familiar with the arrangement of their own infused blood. Other
practitioners could potentially manipulate the blood, but might initially
control it poorly until they understood its arrangement.

This is consistent with the general idea that unfamiliar blood or an
unfamiliar arrangement is harder to control. Whether this applies in a
particular version is writer-defined.

9. BLOOD DOMINION
======================================================================

9.1 CORE IDENTITY

Blood Dominion is a discipline that connects a practitioner's movements
to selected muscle groups in another person. Once activated, the
selected muscle groups mirror the practitioner's corresponding
movements.

The original inspiration was a movement-mirroring idea. The blood mark
is a physical mechanism for establishing the link; it is not an ordinary
shadow and the technique does not manipulate a person's literal shadow.

The detailed mechanics below are the version retained for the current
design. They have deliberately been left unchanged for now.

9.2 SELECTION OF MUSCLE GROUPS

Before activation, the practitioner selects the specific muscle groups
they want to affect.

The practitioner touches the target and places physical blood marks on
the selected muscle groups. Several marks can be placed before the
technique is activated.

The practitioner chooses the exact moment of activation. Activation
locks in the selection. Marks placed after activation do not add new
muscle groups to the technique.

9.3 BLOOD CONNECTIONS

Each mark is linked to the practitioner by a physical stream of blood.
The streams connect the practitioner to the target's marked muscle
groups.

The streams can bend and stretch around obstacles, but only up to a
limit. The exact maximum distance or degree of flexibility has not been
specified.

The technique affects one target at a time.

9.4 MIRRORING

Once activated, each selected muscle group perfectly mirrors the
practitioner's corresponding movements.

The practitioner must physically move the corresponding body parts
for the target's muscles to mirror them. The practitioner does not
simply issue an abstract instruction for the target to perform an
unrelated action.

The mirroring has no built-in adjustment for speed or intensity. The
selected muscles copy the corresponding movements as defined by the
technique.

The target's nervous system is bypassed. The target does not need to
voluntarily perform the movement for the marked muscles to move. The
technique can therefore move muscles even where the target cannot
voluntarily move them due to paralysis or another impairment affecting
voluntary movement. The target's ordinary physical limitations still
apply.

9.5 RESISTANCE

The target can oppose the imposed movement. Resistance is comparable to
trying to move through thick water: the target can make the movement
harder, but cannot simply break the technique completely through that
opposition.

Resistance increases blood consumption.

9.6 FORCE AND BLOOD FLOW

The controlling force is determined by the blood flow or amount supplied
to the target, not by the practitioner's physical strength.

The flow rate is automatically determined by the technique. The
practitioner cannot manually adjust it at will.

9.7 SHARED SUPPLY

The blood supply is shared among all active marks.

All active connections are supplied until the available supply becomes
insufficient. There is no deliberate priority system in which the
practitioner chooses which mark receives blood first.

Higher total demand results in higher total consumption. Movement,
small adjustments, and resistance can change the demand dynamically.
Stillness lowers demand, although it may not eliminate it completely.

If the practitioner has insufficient blood to keep every connection
active, connections fail individually as their needs can no longer be
met. The remaining connections can continue operating.

9.8 INTERRUPTED CONNECTIONS

If a physical blood stream is interrupted, supply to the corresponding
mark stops. However, blood already in the stream continues travelling
for a few seconds.

When that remaining blood reaches the mark, the corresponding muscle
group stops mirroring.

The same grace period applies if the practitioner runs out of blood:
residual blood already travelling through a connection can still reach
its mark before the movement stops.

Interrupting one stream affects only the corresponding muscle group.
It does not automatically deactivate the other connections.

9.9 FAILED MARKS AND REACTIVATION

Once a connection fails, the old mark cannot be reactivated.
A new mark would be required.

However, marks placed after activation do not affect the active
selection. The practitioner must therefore establish any required new
marks before activating a new instance of the technique.

When a connection fails, or when the practitioner ends the technique,
the mark remains as ordinary blood. It no longer functions as an active
Dominion mark.

9.10 SUMMARY OF THE RETAINED MECHANICS

  - One target at a time.
  - The practitioner selects muscle groups before activation.
  - Blood marks are physically placed on those groups.
  - Activation locks in the selected groups.
  - Each mark has a physical blood stream connecting it to the
    practitioner.
  - Streams can bend or stretch around obstacles up to an unspecified
    limit.
  - Selected muscles mirror the practitioner's corresponding
    movements.
  - The practitioner must make the corresponding movements physically.
  - The target's nervous system is bypassed.
  - Physical limitations still apply.
  - The target can resist movement, increasing blood consumption, but
    cannot simply break the technique through resistance.
  - Blood demand is dynamic and automatically distributed.
  - Each connection can fail individually if its supply is insufficient
    or interrupted.
  - Residual blood in a stream gives a short grace period after
    interruption or depletion.
  - Failed marks cannot be reused within the same activation.
  - Marks become ordinary blood when the technique ends.

======================================================================
10. BLOOD COVENANT
======================================================================

10.1 CORE IDENTITY

Blood Covenant is the discipline of creating supernatural agreements
between participants. It makes agreed terms binding through Blood
Magic, allowing violations to be detected and punished without requiring
a human judge or continual human supervision.

The discipline originated conceptually from people who attempted to
stop a great war through contracts and agreements.

The following mechanics are the completed version established in the
design. They should be treated as retained rules unless a writer
intentionally changes the system.

10.2 FORMING A COVENANT

To form a Covenant:

  1. The participants hold hands in a circle.
  2. All participants agree to the terms.
  3. Each participant contributes exactly one drop of their own blood.
  4. The participants activate the technique.
  5. Magical marks appear on the participants' palms.

Each person contributes their own drop. The contributions are not
pooled as a single shared payment before the Covenant is formed.

10.3 COVENANT MARKS

Each participant receives a magical palm mark.

The marks:

  - Can be hidden.
  - Cannot be damaged or removed.
  - Are visually identifiable as Covenant marks.
  - Do not reveal the Covenant's rules to an ordinary observer merely
    because the observer can see the mark.

A marked participant instinctively knows the rules of the Covenant.

Someone using basic Blood Manipulation can reveal the rules by
actively attempting to learn them.

Tampering with a Covenant mark causes excruciating pain to the person
interfering with it. This pain is the established global punishment for
attempted mark tampering.

10.4 TERMS AND SPECIFICITY

Covenant terms must be extremely specific. They should be drafted with
the care of a legal contract.

The technique detects whether the terms have been violated. It does not
require a person to monitor every participant or decide whether a
violation occurred.

Because the Covenant enforces the terms as written, imprecise or
ambiguous wording can create problems. The exact consequences of
ambiguous wording have not been separately defined; a writer may decide
how their version handles it.

10.5 DETECTING AND PUNISHING VIOLATIONS

The Covenant magically detects violations of its terms. Accidental
violations count as violations too, unless the terms themselves make
an exception.

Each violation can have an assigned punishment. Pain is the default
punishment, but a Covenant can specify a different punishment.

There are no supernatural limits on the kinds of punishment that can
be specified beyond the laws of the setting's world and what is
physically possible under those laws.

Two named punishment concepts were established:

  - PAIN: The default punishment for a violation or for tampering with
    the mark.
  - BLOOD DIMINISHMENT: A punishment in which the offender loses blood
    for violating the Covenant.

The precise implementation of punishments is determined by the terms
and by the setting's rules.

10.6 MEMBERSHIP AND JOINING

There is no fixed maximum number of participants. A Covenant can
theoretically include an unlimited number of people.

A new participant can join an existing Covenant by agreeing to its
terms and contributing one drop of their own blood. They receive a
Covenant mark and become bound by its rules.

A participant may belong to multiple Covenants, provided the terms do
not conflict in a way that makes simultaneous compliance impossible.

10.7 LEAVING, RELEASE, AND DEATH

A participant cannot simply choose to leave a Covenant.

A participant remains bound until the Covenant's duration or completion
condition is met, or until they are released by a majority of the other
participants.

The person whose release is being considered does not vote on their own
release.

When released:

  - Their obligations end immediately.
  - Their associated punishments no longer apply.
  - Pending punishments are also removed.
  - Their original blood contribution remains part of the Covenant's
    history or structure; it is not returned merely because they are
    released.

Death is treated exactly like release. A deceased participant is
removed from the Covenant in the same way as a released participant.

Covenants may be indefinite. They do not require a fixed end date if
the terms do not specify one.

10.8 VOTING

Covenant decisions use one vote per participant.

A majority is required. Abstentions are excluded from the count.

A participant who abstains does not contribute a yes or no vote to the
majority calculation.

10.9 THE BLOOD COIN

If a vote ends in a 50-50 tie, a literal Blood Coin forms from blood
associated with that Covenant.

The coin has two sides representing the two possible outcomes. It is
flipped to decide the tied vote.

The coin only forms when a tie occurs. After the result can be read, the
coin vanishes.

The result of the coin flip is final and cannot be challenged for that
vote. However, the participants can hold a new vote later and change
the decision through the ordinary voting process.

10.10 AMENDING TERMS

A majority can amend the Covenant's rules.

Changes take effect immediately. Participants can therefore become
bound by terms that they did not personally vote for, provided the
amendment passed under the Covenant's voting rules.

A change can also retroactively alter the punishment assigned to an
already committed violation, as long as the punishment has not yet
been carried out.

10.11 DISSOLUTION

A Covenant cannot simply be dissolved by a vote while two or more
participants remain.

It disappears when fewer than two participants remain.

This means a Covenant can persist indefinitely while it retains at
least two participants, subject to its terms, releases, deaths, and
other established rules.

10.12 MULTIPLE COVENANTS

An individual can join multiple Covenants. The practical limitation is
that the terms must not conflict in a way that prevents compliance.

The discipline does not impose a separate universal maximum number of
Covenants per participant.

10.13 COVENANTS AS SOCIAL OR LEGAL INFRASTRUCTURE

A world may use Blood Covenant as part of its legal or social system.
For example, a society could teach Covenant to children and require
citizens to enter a Universal Blood Covenant by law. The terms might
restrict forbidden techniques or establish common obligations.

This is an optional worldbuilding application, not a universal feature
of every Blood Magic setting.

======================================================================
11. BLOOD RELIQUARY
======================================================================

11.1 CORE IDENTITY

Blood Reliquary is the discipline that allows a practitioner to store
information about an individual they personally killed using
Blood-based techniques and later reconstruct that individual.

The discipline was inspired in part by the idea of reconstructing
individuals from stored information, but it is not genuine resurrection.

11.2 ELIGIBILITY

The practitioner must personally kill the individual using Blood-based
techniques.

It is not enough that the practitioner merely caused the person's
death through an unrelated means or that the person was killed by
someone else. The established core restriction is that the practitioner
must personally kill the individual using Blood-based techniques.

11.3 STORAGE

The stored individual is held as recorded information in the
practitioner's Reliquary or arsenal, not as a physically preserved
person.

The exact way the information is recorded and stored is writer-defined.

Possible information sources include blood, the practitioner's memory,
a combination of these, or another mechanism defined by the writer.
No single source has been made universal.

11.4 RECONSTRUCTION

The practitioner can later reconstruct an individual from the stored
information.

Reconstruction is a readily available skill within Blood Reliquary by
default. It does not require special external equipment, a separate
ritual object, or an outside summoning device as a universal
requirement.

The exact method of reconstruction is flexible and can be defined by
the writer.

11.5 THE RECONSTRUCTED INDIVIDUAL

A reconstructed individual is not genuinely resurrected. They are a
magical recreation based on stored information.

The established default characteristics are:

  - They retain memories.
  - They retain a slight echo of their original personality.
  - They possess a perfect copy of their skills and techniques.
  - They are compelled to obey the practitioner who reconstructed
    them.
  - They retain their original physical appearance and body.
  - Their body and clothing have a faint, subdued blood-red tint.

The exact degree of independent thought and action is writer-defined.
The general principle is that they are compelled to obey the caster,
but a writer decides how much personality, initiative, interpretation,
and independent thought remain.

11.6 PHYSICAL CAPABILITIES

The recommended default is that reconstructed individuals have the
physical capabilities they had originally, without automatic
enhancement.

A writer may decide otherwise, but enhanced strength, speed, endurance,
or other attributes are not inherent to the discipline's default
identity.

11.7 NUMBER AND DURATION

A stored individual can be reconstructed again; the discipline is not
limited to a single reconstruction of that person. The number of
individuals that can be stored or reconstructed, the capacity of the
Reliquary, the number that can be active at once, and the duration of a
reconstruction are writer-defined.

The design does not impose a universal numerical capacity.

11.8 DAMAGE AND RESTORATION

Damage, repair, restoration, and the consequences of damage are
writer-defined.

An earlier suggested default was that reconstructed individuals could
suffer ordinary bodily damage and would not automatically heal. This
was a proposal rather than a mandatory rule.

11.9 SUMMARY

The defining concept is not immortality or true resurrection. It is the
reconstruction of a person from stored information after the practitioner
has personally killed that person using Blood-based techniques. The
reconstruction retains memories, a trace of personality, and the
person's skills, while remaining compelled to obey the practitioner.

======================================================================
12. BLOOD CRYSTALS AND REPLENISHMENT
======================================================================

12.1 STATUS

Blood crystals are an established optional subsystem from earlier
worldbuilding. They are not required for every version of Blood Magic.
A writer may include them, alter them, or omit them.

12.2 ARTIFICIAL HEART-SHAPED CRYSTALS

The earlier concept allows a small heart-shaped crystal to be created
by placing some of a person's blood in a jar with a stone or crystal.

The difficulty of forming the stone or crystal influences the resulting
replenishment effect. A more difficult-to-form material produces a
greater replenishment effect in the described model.

The exact process, time, limits, and degree of replenishment are not
fully fixed as universal rules.

12.3 NATURALLY OCCURRING HEART-SHAPED CRYSTALS

Naturally occurring heart-shaped crystals are associated primarily with
the area around the Primordial Blood Crystal in the version that uses
that origin.

They can:

  - Fully replenish a practitioner's blood supply.
  - Permanently increase the practitioner's maximum blood capacity.

The earlier model described each natural crystal as adding one
original maximum-capacity increment. If the original capacity is B and
the practitioner has obtained n such crystals, the resulting capacity
is B multiplied by (1 + n). This is additive growth by the original
baseline, not repeated multiplication of the current capacity.

The exact meaning of a person's maximum blood capacity, the number of
crystals available, and the broader consequences of increased capacity
remain open to the writer.

12.4 NOT A MANA-POOL SYSTEM

Even when crystals are included, they do not require Blood Magic to
become a generic points-based resource system. They affect access to
blood and the body's capacity as defined by the writer.

======================================================================
13. LEARNING, RESEARCH, AND SKILL DEVELOPMENT
======================================================================

13.1 LEARNING

The recommended default is that learning Blood Magic combines theory,
study, instinct, and practical experience.

A person may understand the principles of Blood Magic without being
capable of performing the techniques. Understanding and practical
ability are not necessarily the same thing.

Practice can improve control, precision, familiarity, and the ability
to carry out complex techniques.

13.2 TALENT AND ELIGIBILITY

Whether everyone can learn Blood Magic, whether ability is inherited,
whether natural talent matters, and whether some people cannot use it
are writer-defined.

The two origin concepts offer different possible explanations. The
Intrinsic Blood version previously imagined that everyone could learn
with practice. The Primordial Blood Crystal version connected ability
to descent from those whose remains contributed to the crystal.
Neither explanation is mandatory across all settings.

13.3 RESEARCH AND INSTITUTIONS

A setting may contain:

  - Schools that teach Blood Magic.
  - Research institutions.
  - Libraries and written records.
  - Historical discoveries and preserved techniques.
  - Researchers who specialise in theory rather than practical use.
  - Different schools of thought.
  - Famous practitioners and legendary creations.

Books and other records preserve knowledge about Blood Magic. Blood
itself is not a universal storage medium for knowledge, memories, or
written information; any such ability would be an additional
writer-defined property or a specific technique.

13.4 OLD AND NEW KNOWLEDGE

Ancient knowledge can remain useful. Modern practitioners may also
develop techniques or understanding that surpass earlier work.

The system does not require a universal progression ladder or a fixed
ranking structure for practitioners.

======================================================================
14. BLOOD MAGIC IN SOCIETY
======================================================================

14.1 NO UNIVERSAL SOCIAL STATUS

There is no universal rule that Blood Magic must be feared, respected,
rare, common, illegal, or restricted. Its social status depends on the
setting.

An earlier social model imagined Blood Magic as respected and used by
a middle-ground portion of society: many people learned the basics but
pursued other occupations rather than becoming dedicated specialists.
This is an optional example, not a universal population rule.

14.2 EVERYDAY APPLICATIONS

Blood Magic can have practical applications beyond combat, including:

  - Manufacturing and craftsmanship.
  - Medicine and other health-related work, subject to the world's
    ethical and legal rules.
  - Architecture and construction.
  - Multitasking and mechanical assistance.
  - Communication.
  - Transportation.
  - Research, education, and specialist technical work.

The five disciplines may be used in very different ways depending on
the society. Blood Armament, for example, naturally connects to
craftsmanship and manufacturing. Blood Covenant may become part of
law, contracts, and public administration. Other disciplines may have
civilian, professional, or specialist applications beyond their
original inspirations.

14.3 LAWS AND FORBIDDEN TECHNIQUES

Manipulating blood inside living people is generally forbidden in the
default social model. A society may regulate it through ordinary law,
professional standards, religious or cultural practices, or magical
agreements.

An example discussed was a society in which everyone is taught Blood
Covenant when young and is legally required to enter a Universal Blood
Covenant restricting forbidden techniques. This is an example of how
Covenant can become legal infrastructure, not a required feature of the
system.

14.4 PROFESSIONAL SPECIALISATION

Many people may learn basic Blood Magic and then work in other
occupations. Others may specialise in one of the disciplines, research,
teaching, or a technical application.

The system does not dictate which professions must exist or how common
specialists are.

======================================================================
15. INTERACTION WITH MANA-BASED MAGIC
======================================================================

15.1 SEPARATE SYSTEMS

Blood Magic and mana-based magic are distinct systems. Blood Magic
requires physical blood; it does not automatically require mana.

The two systems can interact in particular ways, but one does not
replace the foundations of the other.

15.2 BLOOD ARTILLERY

Blood Artillery has an established broad capacity to interact with
mana-based magic. A projectile may carry a contained mana spell in a
compatible implementation.

The exact mechanics, compatibility, spell count, activation method, and
conditions are writer-defined.

15.3 OTHER INTERACTIONS

Whether other Blood disciplines can interact with mana magic, whether
mana can alter the behaviour of blood, and whether particular objects
or techniques can combine both systems are writer-defined.

15.4 NO AUTOMATIC CONVERSION

The fact that the systems can interact does not imply that blood is
mana, that mana is blood, or that one resource can automatically be
converted into the other.

======================================================================
16. HISTORICAL INSPIRATIONS OF THE FIVE DISCIPLINES
======================================================================

The five formal disciplines were originally connected to five broad
occupational groups among those who died in a gigantic war. These
historical inspirations explain their conceptual lineage, but do not
have to determine every practitioner's role in a later society.

  BLOOD ARTILLERY
  Inspired by cannon firers and cannon operators.

  BLOOD ARMAMENT
  Inspired by warriors and artificers.

  BLOOD DOMINION
  Inspired by people who handled prisoners of war.

  BLOOD COVENANT
  Inspired by people who attempted to stop the war through contracts,
  negotiations, and agreements.

  BLOOD RELIQUARY
  Inspired by medics and generals.

These are the five historical inspirations. They are not mandatory
career paths for modern practitioners.

======================================================================
17. CONSOLIDATED RULE REFERENCE
======================================================================

17.1 UNIVERSAL OR FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

  1. Blood Magic is a system based on physical blood.
  2. Every Blood Magic technique requires physical blood.
  3. General Blood Manipulation is the foundational form of the system.
  4. The system has five formal disciplines: Artillery, Armament,
     Dominion, Covenant, and Reliquary.
  5. Blood availability is a central default limitation.
  6. Excessive use of one's own blood can physically weaken or harm the
     practitioner.
  7. Excessive exertion can cause strain, pain, or technique failure in
     the default model.
  8. Active manipulation requires conscious control by default.
  9. A persistent technique effect does not automatically mean that
     blood is autonomously acting.
 10. Other people's blood can be used by default, though it is
     generally less familiar.
 11. General Blood Manipulation can directly control blood as a fluid.
 12. Blood Armament's defining property is changing the physical
     properties of objects through blood infusion.
 13. Blood Artillery's defining concept is directed blood projectiles.
 14. Blood Dominion's defining concept is movement mirroring through
     blood connections to selected muscle groups.
 15. Blood Covenant's defining concept is magically enforced
     agreements.
 16. Blood Reliquary's defining concept is reconstruction from stored
     information of people the practitioner personally killed using
     Blood-based techniques.

17.2 DEFAULT MECHANICS THAT WRITERS MAY CHANGE

  - Blood can enter and remain inside materials.
  - Blood can exert force from within materials.
  - Material structure and density affect penetration difficulty.
  - Blood can be distributed unevenly and concentrated in selected
    areas.
  - Blood from different sources can be combined.
  - Blood age alone does not automatically make it unusable.
  - Other practitioners may resist or interfere with manipulation of
    their blood.
  - Manipulating blood inside living people is generally forbidden by
    default social and legal norms.
  - Blood Magic can be used spontaneously; preparation is not always
    mandatory.

17.3 WRITER-DEFINED PRINCIPLES

  - The underlying supernatural nature of blood.
  - The origin of Blood Magic.
  - Inheritance, eligibility, and natural talent.
  - Blood's physical and chemical behaviour.
  - Whether blood can transform into other substances.
  - Whether blood retains a supernatural connection to its source.
  - The exact degree of resistance and countering.
  - The exact material compatibility rules.
  - The exact limits of General Blood Manipulation.
  - Most quantitative and operational limits of Blood Artillery.
  - The particular physical properties and additional functions of
    Blood Armament objects.
  - The exact stream limits and other adjustable details of Dominion,
    where not specified above.
  - Reliquary storage, capacity, duration, damage, restoration, and
    independence details.
  - Whether blood crystals exist and how they work.
  - How Blood Magic is learned and organised.
  - How Blood Magic is treated by society and law.
  - How Blood Magic combines with mana-based magic.

19. FINAL DESIGN SUMMARY
======================================================================

Blood Magic is a standalone system in which physical blood is the
required medium for every technique. It is limited by blood availability
and, in the default model, by the physical strain of excessive use.
Direct blood manipulation requires conscious control by default, though
persistent magical effects can remain after the practitioner stops
actively manipulating the blood.

General Blood Manipulation is the foundational form: versatile,
direct control of blood as a fluid. It can move, shape, concentrate, and
manipulate blood, including blood inside living organisms. It is not the
practical specialist for changing the properties of non-blood materials.
That role belongs to Blood Armament.

The five formal disciplines are:

  BLOOD ARTILLERY
  Launches and directs blood projectiles. It can interact with
  mana-based magic, while the exact projectile and spell-payload
  mechanics remain writer-defined.

  BLOOD ARMAMENT
  Infuses crafted materials with blood, allowing blood to change their
  physical properties and giving the objects a connection to their
  creator. The particular properties and additional functions are
  writer-defined.

  BLOOD DOMINION
  Uses physical blood marks and streams to link a practitioner's
  movements to selected muscle groups in one target. The selected
  muscles mirror the practitioner's corresponding movements under the
  detailed rules set out in Chapter 9.

  BLOOD COVENANT
  Creates supernatural agreements whose specific terms are detected
  and enforced by magic. Its rules cover formation, marks, punishments,
  membership, release, voting, the Blood Coin, amendments, and
  dissolution.

  BLOOD RELIQUARY
  Stores information about people the practitioner personally killed
  using Blood-based techniques and later reconstructs them. These are
  not true resurrections; the recreations retain memories, echoes of
  personality, and skills, and are compelled to obey the practitioner.

The system deliberately distinguishes its established foundations
from mechanics that each writer may customise. Its origins, the deeper
nature of blood, many exact limitations, and the wider social role of
Blood Magic remain open to interpretation.

The five disciplines retain distinct identities, while writers remain
free to decide how those identities function in their own worlds.
