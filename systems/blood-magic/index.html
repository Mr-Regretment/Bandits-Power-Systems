PURPOSE AND DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Blood Magic is a standalone magic system built around the manipulation and use of physical blood. It is not a renamed version of a conventional mana system, and it does not require the mechanics or terminology of a game system.

The system has a foundational form of direct blood manipulation and five formal disciplines:

  • - General Blood Manipulation
  • - Blood Artillery
  • - Blood Armament
  • - Blood Dominion
  • - Blood Covenant
  • - Blood Reliquary

General Blood Manipulation is the basic, foundational form rather than a sixth formal discipline. The five named disciplines each have a more specialised identity.

The system is intended to be useful to writers. It therefore establishes clear default foundations while leaving many implementation details to the writer. A writer should be able to use the system as presented without having to invent every rule, but should also be able to adapt particular mechanics to suit a setting or story.

The system is not exclusively a combat system. Its techniques can have applications in craftsmanship, manufacturing, medicine, architecture, transportation, communication, law, research, education, and ordinary daily life, depending on the world in which it exists.

The five disciplines have distinct identities. They should not be interchangeable collections of generic powers. Their exact mechanics may be customised, but their defining concepts should remain recognisable unless a writer deliberately changes the system itself.

No generic mana pool, Blood Unit, spell-slot system, core, arbitrary rank structure, or numerical reserve is required. Blood availability is understood in terms of actual blood and the particular technique being used, rather than a universal points currency.
